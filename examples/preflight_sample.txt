# Sample Manuscript for Preflight Testing

## Introduction
The evolution of linguistic patterns in academic corpora has been extensively analyzed (Gomez, 2019). However, recent studies suggest conflicting methodologies [1, 2].

## Methods
We gathered 1,200 abstracts across four disciplines. The evaluation was carried out using automated natural language parsing tools.
